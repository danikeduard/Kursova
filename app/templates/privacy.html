Privacy

LinguaPath stores your account email, conversation history, ratings, corrections, feedback answers, and bug reports with optional technical details to provide and evaluate the tutoring prototype. Administrators can see the account email associated with feedback and bug reports. Never share sensitive personal information in chat.

Deleting your account removes its feedback, bug reports, conversations, ratings, and other account data. The administrator can see email only for research and support administration.
